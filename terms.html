Welcome to OfMe. These Terms govern your use of the OfMe app and services. By creating an account you agree to these Terms and to the OfMe Privacy Policy.

1. What OfMe is (and is not)

OfMe is a marketplace platform that connects people who want photographs taken ("customers") with independent photographers ("photographers"). OfMe is not a photography studio and does not employ photographers. Photographers are independent contractors who decide when to be available, which jobs to accept, and how far they will travel.

2. Accounts

You must be at least 18, provide accurate information, and keep your phone number current (it is your sign-in). You are responsible for activity on your account. One person per account; you may hold both customer and photographer roles.

3. Booking, pricing, and payment

  • Sessions are sold as fixed-duration packages at the prices shown in the app. Prices are set by OfMe's servers at the moment of booking. Requesting a specific photographer costs more than an open request; the app shows the price before you book.
  • Pro Camera sessions cost more and are provided on a photographer's own professional camera (see Section 7). This tier is not yet available to book.
  • When you book, you authorize OfMe's payment processor, Stripe, to place a hold on your selected payment method. The hold can be up to 25% above the package price so that a tip can be added. After your photos are delivered you are charged the package price, any sales tax that applies, and any tip you add; a cancellation fee, if one applies under Section 4, is charged when you cancel. The unused part of the hold is released. Extending a session in the app is a separate package with its own hold. OfMe does not see or store your full card number.
  • Tips are optional, go 100% to the photographer, and are non-refundable once the session is complete.

4. Cancellations

  • Customers: cancelling is free while we are still searching for a photographer, and free after a photographer accepts as long as they have not set off yet. Once your photographer is on the way, cancelling costs a $5 fee. Once your photographer has arrived at your location, as confirmed by the app's location check, cancelling costs the full package price. A booking with no payment hold on it can always be cancelled free. The app shows the fee, if any, before you confirm a cancellation.
  • Photographers: cancelling an accepted job returns it to other photographers and delays when new jobs unlock for you. The delay is 1 hour for a first cancellation, and 24 hours if it is your second cancellation within 5 days. Three cancellations within 3 days also carries a warning that the account is at risk of probation. A cancelled job cannot be re-taken by the photographer who abandoned it.
  • "Now" jobs a photographer accepts but does not begin within the app's stated window are automatically released to other photographers.
  • OfMe may cancel a job for safety, fraud, or operational reasons.

5. The session

The photographer travels to the customer's chosen location; live location is shared with the customer en route. A session can start only after the app's location check places the photographer at the location. The session lasts the purchased duration and ends automatically; photo capture after the paid window is not accepted by our systems. Both parties agree to behave lawfully and respectfully. Customers must have the right to be photographed at the chosen location; photographers must comply with local rules at the location.

6. Photos: rights and photo privacy

  • Delivery and rights. Photos taken during a session are delivered to the customer's private album. Upon delivery, the photographer assigns to the customer all rights, title, and interest in the session photos and waives any right to retain, use, publish, or exploit them.
  • Photographer lockout. On standard sessions, OfMe's systems remove the photographer's access to the photos upon delivery and delete the in-app copies from their device. Photographers agree not to circumvent this, and doing so is grounds for permanent removal and legal action. No technical measure can stop every deliberate attempt to copy a photo, for example with a second device; OfMe does not guarantee that a photographer who breaks these Terms cannot do so.
  • Photo storage. OfMe stores delivered photos and their technical metadata on our servers to provide your album and operate the service, as described in the Privacy Policy. Users grant OfMe a license to store and use photos for those operational purposes. Photos are removed from our storage when you delete the photo, the album, or your account.
  • Portfolios. Photographer portfolio images must be the photographer's own work with all necessary rights; session photos may never be used in a portfolio.

7. Pro Camera tier: special terms

  • Pro Camera sessions are not yet available to book. When they launch, they are performed on the photographer's own professional camera at a higher price shown in the app.
  • Disclosure: the physical camera stores its own copies of the images, and OfMe cannot erase those. Customers must acknowledge this at booking. Photographers on Pro Camera sessions agree to delete all session images from their camera and any transfer devices after delivery and to never retain, use, or publish them (Section 6 applies equally).
  • Photographers must pass OfMe's camera verification honestly; defeating the verification system is fraud and grounds for permanent removal, and no amount is earned for any session whose photos were not captured live during that session, since the affected customer will be refunded.

8. Photographer earnings

Photographers earn 60% of the package price plus 100% of tips, credited after photo delivery. Payouts are processed through Stripe, our payment processor; completing Stripe's payout onboarding is required to receive funds. OfMe may hold a payout for review when a session's records are incomplete, for example when no photos were delivered or the app could not confirm the photographer was at the location, and releases it once the review is done.

OfMe has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. You can also reach us in-app via "Contact support." We review every report within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these Terms, and remove users who post it or who harass, threaten, or abuse anyone. We may review chat messages, photos, ratings, and reports to enforce these Terms and protect users, and we may suspend or remove any account at our discretion for violations or safety risk.
