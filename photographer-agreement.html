1. Independent contractor

You are an independent contractor running your own business. You are not an employee, agent, partner, or franchisee of OfMe, and you may not tell customers or others that you act for OfMe.

You decide when you are available, which jobs to accept, and how far you will travel. You may decline any job, and declining has no penalty. You may work for anyone else at any time, including our competitors. OfMe does not guarantee any number of jobs or any amount of income and does not require minimum hours.

You decide how to compose and shoot each session. OfMe's rules cover safety, the law, privacy, and how photos are handled. OfMe sets package prices and session lengths, and the app shows what you will earn on a job before you accept it.

You supply and pay for your own phone, data plan, transportation, and other costs of your business; OfMe does not reimburse them. As an independent contractor you do not receive wages, overtime, paid time off, health benefits, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance from OfMe.

2. Approval and eligibility

You must be at least 18, legally able to work in the United States, and accurate and truthful in your application and profile. A person at OfMe reviews your application and decides whether to approve it. We may decline an application, and approval is not a promise of work.

3. Jobs and cancelling

Accepting a job commits you to that session only. Section 4 of the Terms of Service says what happens if you cancel an accepted job. A "now" job you accept but do not begin within the window the app shows is released to other photographers. A customer may request you directly; you may decline, and the request expires if you do not answer within the time the app shows. Failing to appear for an accepted job without notice may lead to removal.

You may refuse or leave any session you reasonably believe is unsafe or unlawful. Tell support right away. If a cancellation penalty applied because of a good-faith safety decision, ask us and we will review it.

4. Pay

For each completed session you earn the amount the app shows you before you accept the job (for an extension, before you start it), plus 100% of any tip. If a customer cancels after the app's location check confirms you arrived, you earn that same amount. OfMe keeps the rest of the package price and any other cancellation fee. There is no fee to join OfMe.

You are paid through Stripe, and you must complete Stripe's onboarding to receive money. Stripe collects your identity, tax, and bank details directly from you. Earnings are paid after the photos are delivered, the tip window has closed, and the customer's payment has been captured. Payouts run in weekly batches, normally on Fridays, and your bank controls when a deposit appears.

We may hold a payout for review when a session's records are incomplete, for example when no photos were delivered or the app could not confirm you were at the location, and we release it once the review is done. We also hold payouts while a customer's card dispute is open, while your account is suspended, or while your bank details need fixing, and we release them when that is resolved.

If a session was not performed as this Agreement requires, or was not performed at all, no amount is earned for it. If a customer is refunded or a card payment is reversed for that reason, we may reverse the related payout or deduct it from later payouts. We will tell you why, and you may ask support to review it.

The session camera blocks screen capture. If a screenshot is detected while the session camera is open, the app ends the session automatically, the customer is not charged, no amount is earned for that session, and your account is suspended until a person at OfMe reviews what happened.

OfMe does not withhold taxes unless the law requires it, for example backup withholding if a valid taxpayer number is not provided. OfMe or Stripe may issue IRS forms such as Form 1099 and report your payments to tax authorities, and some states require us to report contractors we pay. You agree to give accurate tax information. Stripe may ask for your consent to receive tax forms electronically.

You hereby assign to the customer, effective automatically when the session photos are delivered or the session otherwise ends, all right, title, and interest in them, including copyright, and you give up any right to keep, use, publish, or license them. If that assignment is not effective for any reason, you grant the customer an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable license to those photos, or a nonexclusive one if an exclusive license is not effective. The customer may enforce this Section. Your acceptance of this Agreement is your signature on this assignment.

On standard sessions you shoot only in the OfMe app. You will not copy, screenshot, record, or photograph session photos with any other device, and you will not try to get around the photo lockout. Doing so is a serious breach and grounds for permanent removal and legal action. Session photos may not appear in your portfolio or anywhere else. Your portfolio images must be your own work.

7. Conduct and customer information

You will follow the law and any rules at the location, act professionally, and treat customers, their homes, and their property with respect. No harassment, discrimination, threats, or unwanted contact. You will not photograph anyone who does not consent, or a minor unless their guardian consents and is present.

You will use customer names, addresses, and phone numbers only to perform the session and will not keep or share them afterward. You will not ask a customer to pay outside OfMe for a booked session.

While you are marked available, when you open or refresh the Bookings tab, and during a job, the app uses your location as the Privacy Policy describes.

During a session the app tells our servers whether the in-app camera is open. If a customer has turned on the camera alert and the camera goes dark during paid time, both of you are alerted and a report is filed.

8. Insurance

OfMe does not provide insurance for you, your equipment, your vehicle, or your work. You are responsible for carrying any insurance your business needs or the law requires.

9. Ending this Agreement

Either of us may end this Agreement at any time. You can go off duty at any time, and you can leave by deleting your account once any payout owed to you has landed. OfMe may suspend or remove you for a breach of this Agreement or the Terms of Service, a safety risk, repeated cancellations or no-shows, poor reliability, or a legal reason. Amounts you earned for completed sessions stay payable under Section 4, including its holds and reversals. When this Agreement ends you will delete any customer information you still hold.
