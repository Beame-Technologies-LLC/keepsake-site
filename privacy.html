Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

OfMe is a product of Beame Technologies LLC ("OfMe," "we," "us"), which operates a marketplace that connects people who want photos taken ("customers") with nearby photographers. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the OfMe mobile app and this website.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your name, phone number, email address, date of birth (to confirm you are at least 18), and a profile photo when you create an account. Your phone number is verified by a text-message code and is how you sign in.

Profile information (photographers). An optional bio, portfolio photos you choose to upload, your service travel radius, and, if you enable the Pro Camera tier, the make and model of your camera and when it was verified.

Location. For customers: the location you choose for a photo session (typed address or your device location, with your permission). Photographers near a request see only its general area; the photographer who accepts sees the full address. For photographers: while you are marked available, the app reports your location so we can alert you to jobs within your travel radius and show customers who are choosing a photographer your approximate position on a map (rounded to within about half a mile), unless you turn off direct requests in your profile. While you travel to an accepted job, your live location is shared with that customer on a map; the customer can view that route until 24 hours after the session ends, and route points are deleted after 7 days. While you are off duty the app does not report your location in the background. If you open or refresh the Bookings tab, the app sends your current location once so it can show nearby jobs.

Photos and photo details. Photos taken during a session (or, on Pro Camera sessions, imported from the photographer's camera) are delivered to the customer's private album. Each photo is stored with technical details (time taken, device or camera model, image dimensions). See Section 4.

Session camera status. During a session the app tells our servers whether the in-app camera is open. If a customer has turned on the camera alert and the camera goes dark during paid time, both people are alerted and a report is filed.

Messages. In-app chat messages between a matched customer and photographer, including read status.

Support requests and reports. What you send us through "Contact support," reports you file about another user or about content, and our replies (see Section 5 on how replies are drafted).

Transactions. Bookings, package prices, sales tax where it applies, tips, cancellation fees, ratings, and reviews. Card payments are collected and processed by our payment processor, Stripe. OfMe never sees or stores your full card number.

Device information. Push-notification token, device model, operating system version, app version, and basic app diagnostics. If the app or our servers run into an error, a technical crash or error report (what failed, the app version, and the device model and operating system) is sent to our error-monitoring provider so we can fix it. Error reports are not linked to your account and do not include your name, phone number, email address, street address, messages, or photos. We do not use them to track you.

2. How we use information

  • Match customers with nearby photographers and dispatch job alerts.
  • Show live arrival tracking and check the photographer's reported location before a session starts.
  • Deliver photos to the customer and enforce our photographer photo-access lockout (Section 4).
  • Verify Pro Camera equipment: we read the technical metadata inside a proof photo (camera make, model, and time taken) to confirm a real camera is present.
  • Process bookings, tips, fees, and photographer earnings.
  • Calculate the sales tax due on a session from the session's location, where sales tax applies.
  • Answer support requests.
  • Find and fix errors in the app and our servers.
  • Safety, fraud prevention, and dispute resolution.
  • Send service notifications (job status, messages). Marketing messages, if ever sent, will be optional.
  • Comply with law.

3. What other users see

  • Users see each other's first name and last initial only (for example, "Jane D."). Other users never see your full last name.
  • Phone numbers are shared between a customer and a photographer only after the photographer accepts the job, and both of you can still see the number in that booking afterward.
  • Customers see a photographer's public profile: short name, profile photo, bio, portfolio, rating, review history, number of completed sessions, how long they have been a member, an approximate map position while they are available, and (on Pro Camera jobs) the verified camera model.
  • Reviews you post are shown to other signed-in users with your first initial.

4. Photos: ownership, photographer lockout, and storage

Your photos go to you. Photos taken in a session are delivered directly to the booking customer's private album.

The photographer loses access. When a session's photos are delivered, the photographer's access to them is revoked by our servers and the app removes its copies from their device. On standard sessions the service is designed so that photographers do not keep your photos. No technical measure can stop every deliberate attempt to copy a photo, for example with a second device; doing so breaks our Terms.

Pro Camera exception, disclosed at booking. On Pro Camera sessions the photographer uses their own physical camera, which stores its own copies that OfMe cannot erase. The app requires you to acknowledge this before booking a Pro Camera session.

How we store your photos. Your delivered photos and their technical details are stored on our servers so we can provide your private album and operate the service. Access is limited to authorized OfMe personnel, and to automated systems OfMe operates, for support, safety, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We keep your delivered photos until you delete them or delete your account. When you delete a photo, an album, or your account, those image files are removed from our storage right away; a profile photo can take up to 30 days to be removed. Our encrypted database backups, which hold records about photos but not the image files, are replaced on a rolling schedule, so those records are gone from every backup within 30 days. If a photographer deletes their account, photos already delivered to a customer stay in that customer's album.

How long we keep other information. Route points from a job are deleted after 7 days. Messages, support requests, and profile information are kept until you delete your account. After an account is deleted we keep a record of completed bookings and payments (the date, the package, and the amount) with your name and contact details removed, because financial records must be kept for tax and accounting.

5. Who we share information with

  • The other party to your job, as described in Section 3.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us: database and file storage (Supabase), text-message sign-in codes (Twilio), push notifications (Expo, Apple, and Google), address search and maps (Google and Apple, and Photon by Komoot as a backup for address search; these receive the address text you type and the general area you are searching in), payment processing and photographer payouts (Stripe, which also receives the session location to calculate sales tax where it applies), error monitoring (Sentry), and AI drafting of support replies (described below).
  • Support replies drafted with AI. Some replies to support requests are drafted by AI software, either on computers OfMe operates or through an outside AI provider (Anthropic) under contract, which then receives the text of your support request. Routine replies may be sent automatically. Replies that involve a refund, money, or a concern about your photos are designed to be held for a person at OfMe to review before they are sent. You can always ask for a person to handle your request.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the safety of users or the public.
  • Business transfers: if OfMe is acquired or merges, information may transfer with the business.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Location is controlled by your device permissions; photographers can go off-duty at any time.
  • Notifications are controlled in your device settings.
  • Access and deletion: contact us (Section 10) to request a copy or deletion of your data. You can also delete your account yourself in the app (Profile, then Delete my account), which removes your session photos from our storage right away; this page explains what is deleted and what is kept, and Section 4 explains backups.

7. Children

OfMe is not directed to children under 13, and users must be at least 18 to create an account.

8. Security

Photos are kept in private storage with per-job access rules, access to records is enforced by our database and not only by the app, and data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information.

9. Changes and disputes

We will post changes here and update the date above; material changes will be announced in the app. Disputes about this policy are resolved under Sections 16 and 17 of our Terms of Service, which include binding arbitration and a class action waiver.
