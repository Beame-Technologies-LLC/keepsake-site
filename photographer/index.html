OfMe is photography on demand. You pick a spot and a session length, a nearby photographer heads to you, and the photos land in your private album in the app as they are taken. The photographer never keeps a copy.

How a session works, anywhere

1

Request

Drop a pin where you already are and pick a length, from 5 minutes to a full hour.

2

Track

A nearby photographer accepts and heads your way. You watch them arrive on a live map.

3

Shoot and receive

Photos are taken in the app and land in your album as they are shot. Rate your photographer, tip if you loved it.

If nobody nearby is available, the request expires on its own, you are not charged, and you can try again later. That is the honest version of on demand: it depends on who is out working near you at that moment.
