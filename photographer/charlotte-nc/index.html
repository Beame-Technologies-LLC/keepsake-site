Charlotte spots that photograph well

  • Romare Bearden ParkUptown's best skyline angle without needing a rooftop. The lawn puts the towers behind you and it holds up after dark.
  • The Rail Trail, South EndMurals, brick, and the light rail going past. It is the closest thing Charlotte has to a built-in backdrop, and it is a walk rather than a destination, so a 10 minute session covers a lot of it.
  • Freedom ParkThe default for family photos in this city for a reason: the lake, the bandstand, the bridge, and enough space that a dog can be in the shot without being on a leash-length crop.
  • Little Sugar Creek GreenwayRuns from Uptown down past Freedom Park. Good when you want green and shade in July and do not want to drive anywhere.
  • NoDa, along North DavidsonPainted walls, old mill buildings, and a stretch where every fifty feet looks different. Best on a weekday, because the weekend crowd walks through every frame.
  • Camp North EndIndustrial concrete, big murals, and hard shade. It suits headshots and content days better than it suits a family of six.
  • The First Ward and The GreenSmall Uptown parks that work when you have fifteen minutes between meetings and need something that is not a conference room wall.
  • Latta Park, DilworthOld trees and porches around the edges. In the last two weeks of March and the first of April the whole neighborhood is in bloom.
  • U.S. National Whitewater CenterWater, bridges, and trail. Worth an hour session rather than five minutes, and worth checking their access and parking rules before you go.
  • The airport overlookPlanes low over the fence at Charlotte Douglas. A niche shot that kids remember longer than the posed one at the park.

A note that saves people money: public sidewalks and public parks are fine for a short handheld session, but private gardens, ticketed venues, and malls set their own photography rules and some of them charge. Check before you drop the pin there.

What people shoot in Charlotte

Headshots, because this is a banking town

Charlotte runs on LinkedIn photos. A 10 minute session outside your building in Uptown or in South End gets you a current headshot without booking a studio or losing a morning. Take the pin to the shady side of the street and go before 10am.

Family photos

Freedom Park in the fall, Latta Park in early spring, or your own front yard, which is genuinely the most underrated option in a city with this much tree canopy. Twenty minutes is usually the right length for a family with small kids. An hour is not.

Graduation

UNC Charlotte, Queens, Johnson C. Smith, Davidson, Central Piedmont. Campus grounds are the obvious backdrop and most schools have rules about commercial shoots during graduation weekend, so the sidewalk outside the gates and a good landmark nearby is often the easier call.

Dogs

Freedom Park, the greenway, or the patio where they already know your dog's name. Short sessions suit dogs. Five to ten minutes is about as long as most of them will cooperate anyway.

Proposals

Romare Bearden Park at dusk with the skyline behind, or the quiet end of Freedom Park. Because OfMe photographers arrive as themselves rather than as an obvious hired crew standing around waiting, the setup is less conspicuous than a scheduled shoot.

Game day and event nights

Uptown around the stadium and the arena. Short sessions in a crowd are what a five or ten minute package is built for.

Charlotte questions

Can I book an OfMe photographer in Charlotte today?

Not yet. OfMe is coming to the App Store and Charlotte is one of the first areas we are opening. We are not publishing a launch date until we can hold it.

Which parts of Charlotte can I request from?

You set the pin, so a request can come from anywhere in the metro, Uptown to Ballantyne to Davidson. Whether a photographer accepts depends on who is available near that pin at that moment, which is the honest answer for any on-demand service.

What happens if no photographer accepts?

The request expires on its own, you are not charged, and you can try again later. Your card is only authorized at booking, never charged for a session that never happened, and that authorization releases on its own.

How much does a photo session in Charlotte cost?

From $9.99 for 5 minutes to $64.99 for a full hour, the same as every other OfMe area. That is the standard price list. Requesting one specific photographer by name costs 1.5 times the list price, and extending at the end of your session is priced at 10 percent off the extra time.

Do I need a permit at a Charlotte park or garden?

Public sidewalks and most public parks are fine for a short handheld session. Private gardens, ticketed venues, malls, and some managed public spaces set their own photography rules and a few of them charge. Check the venue before you set the pin there.

Can I book after dark?

There is no cutoff time in the app. Uptown holds up at night because the towers and the park lawns give you something to work with. An unlit greenway at 10pm is a harder shot and your photographer will say so.

Does the photographer keep my photos?

No. Photos never reach their camera roll, and their access is revoked the moment your session is delivered. See Support for how delivery, refunds, and reporting work.
