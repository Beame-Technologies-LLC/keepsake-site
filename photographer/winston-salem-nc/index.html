Winston-Salem spots that photograph well

  • Old SalemBrick walks, plain Moravian buildings, and a street that looks the same in every decade. The strongest backdrop in the city and the one everybody's grandmother will recognize. Check the property's rules before you plan a posed session on the grounds.
  • Reynolda GardensFormal beds, the greenhouse, and the long walk from Reynolda Village. It photographs best in late spring and again in October, and it is a five minute drive from Wake Forest.
  • Bailey Park and the Bailey Power PlantLawn, brick, and those two smokestacks. The Innovation Quarter is the one part of downtown built to be walked through, which suits a short session.
  • Trade Street and the downtown arts districtMurals, storefronts, and a lot of texture in three blocks. Weekday afternoons are calmer than First Friday.
  • Quarry ParkThe overlook that gives you the downtown skyline from outside downtown. Not many cities this size have a view of themselves.
  • Salem LakeWater, dock, and a long shaded trail. Good for dogs, good for engagement photos, and good in August when everywhere else is baking.
  • Tanglewood Park, ClemmonsThe covered bridge, the rose garden, and enough room for a whole family that arrived in three cars. Worth the twenty minute drive from downtown.
  • Historic Bethabara ParkThe 1753 Moravian settlement site, with the log church, the gardens, and the fields. Quieter than Old Salem and often empty on a weekday.
  • Washington Park and Miller ParkNeighborhood parks with real trees. This is where you go for a twenty minute family session that does not turn into an expedition.
  • The Reynolds Building, downtownThe art deco tower locals will tell you was the prototype for the Empire State Building. Shoot it from the street corner and let the vertical do the work.

The practical note: public sidewalks and public parks are fine for a short handheld session. Historic properties, private gardens, and ticketed venues set their own photography rules and some of them charge for a session. Ask first, because the street outside often gets you ninety percent of the look for nothing.

What people shoot in Winston-Salem

Senior portraits and graduation

Wake Forest, UNC School of the Arts, Winston-Salem State, Salem College, Forsyth Tech. Five campuses in one small city means May and December are the busiest photo weeks of the year here. Campus rules on posed sessions vary, so the classic answer is the gates, the sign, and a good spot ten minutes away.

Family photos and holiday cards

Old Salem in December and Tanglewood in October carry the card by themselves. Book twenty minutes rather than an hour if there are children under six involved. Everybody is happier.

Engagements and proposals

Reynolda Gardens, the Salem Lake dock, or a quiet corner of Bethabara. Because an OfMe photographer shows up as one more person on the path rather than a crew with light stands, it stays a surprise until it does not need to be.

Arts and audition portfolios

A city with a conservatory in it has steady demand for dance, music, and theater headshots. Downtown brick and the Innovation Quarter give you clean backgrounds without a studio rental.

Fall color

Late October into early November is the two week window here, and it is worth planning around. Reynolda, Tanglewood, Salem Creek Greenway, or your own street if you happen to live under an old oak.

Dogs

Salem Lake trail and the neighborhood parks. Five or ten minutes is usually all the cooperation you are getting, and that is exactly the size of the smallest package.

Winston-Salem questions

Can I book an OfMe photographer in Winston-Salem today?

Not yet. OfMe is coming to the App Store and Winston-Salem is one of the first areas we are opening. We are not publishing a launch date until we can hold it.

Which parts of the area can I request from?

You set the pin, so anywhere from downtown to Clemmons to Kernersville. Whether a photographer accepts depends on who is available near that pin at that moment, which is the honest answer for any on-demand service.

What happens if no photographer accepts?

The request expires on its own, you are not charged, and you can try again later. Your card is only authorized at booking, never charged for a session that never happened, and that authorization releases on its own.

How much does a photo session in Winston-Salem cost?

From $9.99 for 5 minutes to $64.99 for a full hour, the same as every other OfMe area. That is the standard price list. Requesting one specific photographer by name costs 1.5 times the list price, and extending at the end of your session is priced at 10 percent off the extra time.

Can I shoot at Old Salem or Reynolda Gardens?

Both are historic properties with their own visitor and photography rules, and the rules for a posed session are usually stricter than for a walk with your phone out. Ask them first. The public streets around both often get you the same look with no paperwork.

When is the best time of year for outdoor photos?

Late October into early November for fall color, and late March into April for dogwoods and azaleas. In July and August, book the first hour after sunrise or the last hour before sunset.

Does the photographer keep my photos?

No. Photos never reach their camera roll, and their access is revoked the moment your session is delivered. See Support for how delivery, refunds, and reporting work.
